## Verified finding records

### SSE-01

- **Failure scenario:** A reader frames a nonterminal batch while no waiter exists; a writer then commits later deltas and notifies. Without an outer loop and immediate re-query, live delivery can hang or become heartbeat-bound.
- **Final-TSD location:** §18.
- **Repository/review fact:** The app currently has no `/stream`, `StreamingResponse`, `EventSource`, or `text/event-stream` implementation. Phase 5 §14 contains the missing `while streaming` structure.
- **Resolution:** §18 now makes SQLite authoritative, uses a stable per-run condition only as a wakeup optimization, re-queries after every nonterminal batch and every wakeup, uses `notify_all()`, and specifies reconnect, restart, terminal, stale-cursor, spurious-wakeup, and condition-lifetime behavior.
- **Implementation blocker:** Yes for unsupervised M6 planning; not an architecture blocker.

### CLAIM-01

- **Failure scenario:** The old “structured direction/time” wording required a planner to invent fields, parse arbitrary claim prose, or silently omit a named check.
- **Final-TSD location:** §13.
- **Repository/review fact:** The current Validator performs a string-oriented structured-direction check and retains an LLM repair call; Frozen ClaimPlan and Frozen ClaimValidator do not require a direction field or semantic prose parser.
- **Resolution:** §13 limits ClaimValidator to machine-checkable identities, typed Analyst-declared bindings, deterministic ceilings/relations, citations, limitations, and hashes. Semantic support, contradiction, causal relevance, magnitude fit, direction, and time-scope inference remain EvidenceAnalyst-owned. No new field or model call was added.
- **Implementation blocker:** Yes for M2 schema planning; not a Frozen blocker.

### API-01

- **Failure scenario:** Workbench/tests could bind Frozen `sequence`/`emitted_at` while app code emitted Phase-7 `seq`/`occurred_at`, splitting replay and dedup contracts.
- **Final-TSD location:** §§17 and 21.
- **Repository/review fact:** Frozen §8.2 and Phase 5 §13.1 use `sequence` and `emitted_at`; prior Phase 7 wording exposed `seq` and `occurred_at`.
- **Resolution:** §17 restores Frozen public names and permits different internal SQLite column names only behind the app serializer. §21 restates `packages/app` as the sole live public DTO/projection boundary and restricts Workbench to public DTOs, RunEvents, and safe projections.
- **Implementation blocker:** Yes for M2 public event contracts.

### AGENT-01

- **Failure scenario:** A Writer or UI could treat `ABSTAIN + EVIDENCE_BACKED_CAUSAL` as an evidence-backed causal finding even though no cause was accepted.
- **Final-TSD location:** §§10, 13, and 21.
- **Repository/review fact:** Frozen defines status and type as orthogonal and defines ABSTAIN as inability to make a responsible attribution. The Final TSD already stated the ontology but did not force WriterInput/UI projection behavior.
- **Resolution:** §13 requires the fixed abstention output path regardless of type and forbids causal-finding or NO_MATERIAL language. §21 suppresses attribution-type badges for ABSTAIN and renders the supplied gaps/limitations. The 2/3 logical call budget is unchanged.
- **Implementation blocker:** Yes for M5 Writer planning.

### DATA-01

- **Failure scenario:** M3 could apply Frozen's 90% SEC parse gate to section-boundary quality and never exit at the current 0%, or redefine the denominator after seeing results.
- **Final-TSD location:** §5.3 and M3.
- **Repository/review fact:** The promoted snapshot contains 5,229 filing rows, 1,659 successful non-empty filing documents, and 131,180 official-government serving chunks with `section_parse_degraded=1` for all 131,180.
- **Resolution:** §5.3 binds a predeclared benchmark-accession denominator and a numerator requiring successful non-empty hash-bound primary-document extraction plus authoritative accepted-time or declared fail-closed `eligible_at`. Section degradation remains a separate reported constraint and cannot silently redefine the 90% gate.
- **Implementation blocker:** Yes for M3 promotion; not for M1.

### DATA-02

- **Failure scenario:** News cases retrieve titles only, honestly produce PARTIAL/ABSTAIN, and are then misreported as model/Analyst failures rather than coverage limitations.
- **Final-TSD location:** Existing §§5.2, 5.3, 22, and 29.
- **Repository/review fact:** The promoted serving relation contains 30,945 metadata-only and 21 active reported-news chunks.
- **Resolution:** No architecture patch. M3/M7 writing plans must recover full bodies for the declared Stage-1/2 window or classify affected cases and denominators as coverage-limited, not model-limited. Metadata/title-only text remains lead-only and cannot support complex causal claims.
- **Implementation blocker:** No for design; data-gating blocker for credible M7 news-attribution numbers.

### RUNTIME-01

- **Failure scenario:** Phase 5's two workers plus four pending tasks could be inherited as six admitted runs, violating Frozen's 2–4 capacity bound.
- **Final-TSD location:** §§11, 15, 16 and M6.
- **Repository/review fact:** Phase 5 §7.2 explicitly stated total admission capacity 6; the prior Final-TSD wording repeated “two workers and four pending capacity tokens.”
- **Resolution:** The authoritative invariant is `admission_slots in [2, 4]`, `max_workers <= admission_slots`, and `ACCEPTED + RUNNING + CANCEL_REQUESTED <= admission_slots`. There is no additive pending pool. A slot is released only after a terminal commit or pre-visibility admission rollback, and startup reconciles durable capacity-bearing rows before reopening admission.
- **Implementation blocker:** No architecture blocker; mandatory before M6 runtime planning.
